###WHAT NOT TO DO###

- **DO NOT OMIT SAFETY INFORMATION OR WARNINGS**; THIS IS A VIOLATION OF SECTION 6.3 OF THE STANDARD

- **AVOID UNCLEAR LANGUAGE, ACRONYMS, OR IN-HOUSE TERMINOLOGY** WITHOUT DEFINITIONS
- **DO NOT IGNORE FORMAT GUIDELINES**; IFU SHOULD BE CONSISTENT AND SUITABLE FOR THE DELIVERY MEDIUM
- **NEVER MIX MARKETING CONTENT WITH TECHNICAL INSTRUCTIONS**
- **DO NOT NEGLECT USER FEEDBACK**; A CORE REQUIREMENT OF 82079 IS VALIDATION AND REVISION BASED ON USER INTERACTION
- **AVOID DUPLICATION AND DISORGANIZATION** THAT CONFUSE USERS OR HINDER TASK COMPLETION
